# Promotion: the countdown

Promotion gives people a reason to sign up now and reminds those who meant to. Draft each piece, show it, and post or send only on the organiser's yes. Hand the posts to the social media team (`social-media/guide.md`) with the brief and this plan.

## The countdown

| Beat | When (full plan) | What it says | Format ideas |
|---|---|---|---|
| Announcement | 4+ weeks out | What, when, where, who for, what you leave with, how to sign up | Short video of the host or the space; carousel of the details |
| Line-up or detail reveals | Weekly after the announcement | One new reason to come: a speaker, a menu, a material, a song | One post per reveal; speaker clip with their consent |
| Early-bird deadline | 2-3 days before it ends | The real end date and the price after | Single image with the date |
| Social proof | 2 weeks out | Real quotes, photos or numbers from past events or current sign-ups, with permission | Quote card; photo carousel |
| Last chance | Final week, only if places are limited and remaining | The true number of places left, or "sign-ups close on [date]" | Story or short post |
| Registrant reminders | A week before and the day before | Practical details, not selling | Email or message from the sign-up tool |

In the compressed plan, merge the announcement and the first reveal, and skip the early-bird.

## Channels

| Channel | How to use it | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Own email list | Announcement, one reminder, last chance | Usually the strongest channel; send only to people who agreed |
| Own social accounts | The countdown beats | One platform done well beats three done thinly |
| Local listings | Community calendars, venue listings, city event sites | Submission deadlines vary: check each one |
| Partners | Venue, speakers, sponsors, related businesses | Give them a ready blurb, image and link; ask, do not assume |
| Communities | Groups and forums where the audience already talks | Follow each group's rules; post once, answer questions |
| Local press | A short release or a personal note to one reporter | Lead with what is new or local; send weeks ahead |
| Direct invitations | Personal messages to people who would love it | Slow but effective for small events |

Paid promotion needs a stated budget and the organiser's yes, and only after the page converts organic visitors.

## Partner kit

Give every partner the same small kit:

- Two or three sentences about the event, in plain words.
- The link (tracked, so sign-up sources can be read later).
- One square and one vertical image.
- Date, time, place, price.
- A line they can post as is.

## Honest urgency

Urgency must be true. The compliance reviewer checks each of these.

| Allowed | Not allowed |
|---|---|
| "6 places left" when 6 are left | "Almost sold out" when it is not |
| "Early-bird ends Friday 21 February" and it ends then | Extending a deadline after saying it was final |
| "120 people have signed up" from the sign-up tool's count | Inventing or rounding up attendance |
| Quotes from past attendees with their permission | Made-up or anonymous testimonials presented as real |
| Photos of past events with consent | Stock crowds presented as this event |

## Reading what works

Use a tracked link per channel, so the sign-up tool shows where sign-ups came from. After each beat, note sign-ups that day and their source. If a channel brings nothing after two posts, move the effort to the one that works.

## Beginner mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Announcing without a working sign-up | Run the end-to-end test first |
| Every post says "sign up now" | Each beat gives a new reason |
| Posting the same text everywhere | Adapt to each channel's format and audience |
| Promoting only on social media | Own list and partners usually fill rooms |
| Forgetting people who already signed up | Reminders with practical details |
