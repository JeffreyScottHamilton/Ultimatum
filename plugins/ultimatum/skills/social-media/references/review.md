# Review

Read before showing a batch for approval.

Run `ult posts plan social/plan.json` (and `ult posts check <file> --platform <channel> --caption-file <f>` on each finished image or video), fix every blocker, then go through this list. Quote the summary line in the report.

## Claims

- [ ] Every claim, price, date and number comes from the brief or the user. Nothing invented, rounded up or "about".
- [ ] Health, money, results and before-and-after claims are ones the owner can back and are allowed for their trade; when unsure, soften or cut and say why.
- [ ] Quotes and reviews are real, with the person's permission to be shown; no fake or paid-for reviews presented as ordinary ones.

## Disclosure

- [ ] Paid, gifted, affiliate and employee posts say so at the start of the caption in plain words ("Ad", "Paid partnership with ...", "Gifted by ...") and use the platform's paid-partnership label where it has one (`paid_label: true` in the plan). A hashtag buried at the end is not enough. Rules differ by country: name the country and look up the advertising regulator's current guidance before a paid campaign.
- [ ] Realistic images, video or voices made or changed with AI carry the platform's AI label (`ai_label: true`), and are never presented as a real customer, event or person.

## Rights

- [ ] Every photo, clip, font and sound is the user's own, licensed for this use, or from the platform's own commercial library. Write where each came from. "Found online" is not a licence.
- [ ] People who can be recognised agreed to be shown; children only with a parent's written yes.
- [ ] Competitor posts were studied for patterns only; no words, footage or audio were copied.

## Access

- [ ] Alt text on every image, captions on every video with speech, CamelCase hashtags, no meaning carried by colour alone, text on images large and high-contrast.

## Links and numbers

- [ ] Every link opens, lands on the right page and carries `utm_source` and `utm_campaign`.
- [ ] No post promises an offer, price or date the site does not show.

Fixes are re-checked with `ult posts plan` before the approval request.
