# Intake: online store

Goal: enough to write a one-page brief the user signs off, in few turns. Ask decisions in batches of 5-8 with AskUserQuestion: mostly multiple choice, your **recommended answer first** and labelled "(recommended)". Ask "why" questions one at a time in chat. Skip what files or earlier answers already say. If the user says "I don't know", suggest an answer and mark it `(guess)` in the brief.

Prices, fees and vendor terms you offer as options go through the fact-finder first and are marked "check before relying" until read on the vendor's page.

## Batch 1: the store

| Question | Options | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| What do you sell? | Physical goods you hold / made to order / print-on-demand / digital downloads / a mix | The simplest one that matches today |
| How many products at launch? | 1-5 / 6-30 / 31-200 / more | Launch small: 5-30 best sellers |
| Which platform? | Shopify / WooCommerce / other / not sure | Shopify unless they already run WordPress (see `route.md`) |
| Where are your customers? | One country / a few countries / worldwide | Start with the one or two you can ship to well |
| Who ships? | You / a 3PL warehouse / a POD partner / dropship supplier | Whoever does it today |

## Batch 2: money and rules

| Question | Options | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| Payments | Platform's own payments / Stripe / PayPal / other | The platform's own, plus PayPal if customers ask |
| Tax situation | Registered for sales tax or VAT / not yet / not sure | Not sure: flag it for an accountant (not tax advice) |
| Returns | 14-30 days free / customer pays return / final sale | A clear 30 days (rule of thumb, not a law) |
| Existing channels | Etsy / Amazon / Instagram / farmers market / none | Keep them; point them at the new store |
| Launch date | A date / an event or season / when it is good | A date 3-5 weeks out, after a test-order week |
| Monthly budget for apps, ads, tools | Under 100 / 100-500 / more / none | Ask the real ceiling; prices: check before relying |

## Why-questions, one at a time

1. Why a store of your own, and why now (Etsy fees, brand control, a new product)?
2. Why would someone buy from you instead of the big marketplace?
3. What would "it worked" be in 90 days (orders per month, revenue, email list size)?

## Also ask (free text)

- 3 competitor stores and what you do better.
- 3-5 stores whose look you like, and one thing about each.
- Do you have product photos? Are they consistent (same background, angles, light)? If not, plan a shoot from `product-photos.md` before the build.
- Real footage of the maker or customers, with permission to use it?
- Do refund, shipping, privacy and terms pages exist already? If not, they go on the plan (`policy-pages.md`).
- Company size (more than 3 people changes video tool licences; see `checkout-and-ops.md`).

## How answers fill the brief

| Brief part | Comes from |
|---|---|
| Audience | Customer location, competitors, why-question 2 |
| Offer (with price) | What is sold, catalogue size, price range, first offer |
| Main visitor action | Add to cart and checkout (email signup as the second action) |
| Pages | Home, collection, product, cart, about, contact, policies, FAQ, shipping |
| Known details | Platform, fulfilment, payments, tax status, returns, date, budget |
| Open questions | Every `(guess)`, tax and shipping rates not yet confirmed |
| Checks | The 90-day number, plus: test order placed, policies live, mobile checkout works |

Below the brief add: route and platform picked and why, reference stores, existing channels, who does the owner-only steps.

Voice, colours and story belong to Business's brand interview after approval; pass it your answers so it asks only what is missing.
