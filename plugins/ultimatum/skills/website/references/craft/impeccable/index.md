# Impeccable: index

Read when a page or app screen needs auditing, critique, hardening, microcopy, adapting, refining or native platform rules beyond what Taste, Hallmark, Emil motion and design review QA already hold.

Source: pbakaus/impeccable at commit 3c5e75f, Apache-2.0. The native iOS and Android notes in the source are MIT text derived from another project; ours are rewritten from Apple's and Google's own guidelines. All wording here is our own.

- `audit-scorecard.md`: how do I score a page on five areas out of 20 and rank what to fix?
- `critique.md`: how do I run a heuristic, cognitive-load and persona critique?
- `harden.md`: does it survive long text, other languages, errors, empty states and interrupted gestures?
- `microcopy.md`: are the buttons, labels, errors and empty states written to be understood?
- `refine-moves.md`: should I polish, simplify, tone down or turn up, and in what order?
- `adapt-contexts.md`: how does it change for touch, tablet, desktop, print and email?
- `native-platforms.md`: what must an iOS or Android app do to feel native?
- `direction-process.md`: where does each truth live, what do I ask, and how do I test a direction?
- `surface-details.md`: which type, colour-role and spacing details are still missing?
- `delight-and-onboarding.md`: where do delight and motion belong, and how should first use teach?
- `ambitious-tech.md`: which heavy browser technique fits, and how do I keep it fast and accessible?
- `later.md`: what was left out, and what would it take?

The example page and its browser check are in `../../../../../data/craft/impeccable/examples/`.
