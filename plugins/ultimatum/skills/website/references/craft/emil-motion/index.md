# Emil motion: index

Read first when a task involves animation, transitions, gestures or UI polish on a website or web app. One line per file with the question it answers. Tables are in `../../../../../data/craft/emil-motion/` (see its README). Where house rules exist for marketing pages (`../taste/motion.md`), they win; the files here go deeper on product UI.

Written in our own words from the ideas in the MIT-licensed emilkowalski/skills (commit 715c180). No source text or code is copied.

- `gate.md`: should this move at all, and what should go?
- `easing-and-duration.md`: which curve, how long, and why exits are faster?
- `springs.md`: when does a spring beat a curve, and how do velocity and momentum carry over?
- `components.md`: how do I build the motion of a button, popover, dialog, toast, accordion or reveal?
- `choreography.md`: how do several things move together, in what order, and with what gaps?
- `gestures.md`: how do drag, swipe, sheet and hold gestures track and release?
- `performance.md`: why do frames drop and which properties are safe?
- `reduced-motion.md`: what changes for people who ask for less motion, and what about hover on touch?
- `review-and-audit.md`: how do I review, audit or write a fix plan for existing motion?
- `vocabulary.md`: what is this effect called, and what is it often confused with?
- `ui-details.md`: which one-line fixes make a screen look finished (numbers, tracking, gradients, plurals)?
- `apple-fluid.md`: what principles make an interface feel physical, and how do translucent surfaces work?
- `mobile-web.md`: why does the page feel wrong on a phone, and what fixes it?
- `worst-case-data.md`: how do I stress a component with realistic worst-case data?
- `prototype-variants.md`: how do I show several different versions and let the user pick?
- `libraries-and-toasts.md`: which library fits, and how do toasts work and fail?
- `feedback-and-laws.md`: did my click land, where did that panel come from, and why does this target, menu or wait feel wrong (Fitts, Hick, Doherty)?
- `later.md`: what is left for native apps, Swift and tooling?
