# Ultimatum Event Launch

A launch manager for public events: workshops, classes, shows, meetups, openings, pop-ups, webinars, fundraisers and small conferences. It works back from the event date, from intake to the follow-up after the event. Nothing is posted, sent or paid for without your approval.

## Install

```
/plugin marketplace add JeffreyScottHamilton/Ultimatum
/plugin install ultimatum-event-launch@ultimatum
```

## Start

Say "help me fill my pottery workshop on 14 March" in chat, or type `/ultimatum-event-launch:launch <your event and date>`. Progress is kept in the project, so you can stop and resume.

## What it covers

- An event intake in short rounds: type, date, format, capacity, price, audience, list, budget, venue, speakers and the goal.
- A one-page brief you approve before anything is made.
- A route by event type and lead time: sign-up tool, your own page or a listing platform, and the video style.
- A timeline back from the date (eight weeks or more, four, two, launch week, the day before, the day, the week after), and a compressed plan when time is short.
- Tickets and sign-ups: free, paid or donation, tiers, capacity and waitlist, refund and transfer policy in plain words, the form, confirmation and reminder messages, and a test purchase.
- A countdown promotion plan with honest urgency only.
- A day-of run sheet, a pre-announce checklist and a results review after the event.

## Out of scope

Weddings, private parties and festival logistics. For those it helps only with public-facing parts, such as an announcement page.

## Notes

Venue, permits, licences, insurance, food, alcohol and capacity appear as checklist items marked "check locally"; the plugin does not say what the law requires. Prices, fees and platform terms are marked "check before relying". Confirm them with the venue, the local authority or the vendor.
