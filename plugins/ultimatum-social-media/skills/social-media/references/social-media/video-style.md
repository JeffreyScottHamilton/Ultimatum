# Video style router

Pick the style once per video, before briefing anyone. Ask these five questions of the post and the offer:

1. **Core message.** A feeling or a belief (why we exist, what it is like) points to **film** (the Brand Film guide (`../brand-film/guide.md`)). A feature, a mechanism or a number points to **motion graphics** (the Motion Graphics guide (`../motion-graphics/guide.md`)).
2. **People.** If real people carry the story (founder, customer, team), film. If the product screen or a diagram carries it, motion.
3. **Goal.** Awareness: one hero film, then cutdowns for each channel. Conversion: several motion-graphics variants, each testing one hook.
4. **Update rate.** Frequent updates (new features, prices, languages) favour motion; a film is costly to redo.
5. **Budget and access.** Film needs real people, footage or a generator budget. If those are missing, motion is the realistic choice.

**A person talking to camera** (founder, creator, expert, a podcast or stream to clip) carrying the point is a third style: **creator cut** (the Creator Cut guide (`../creator-cut/guide.md`)). It is the cheapest to make often: one recording, several shorts.

Most answers agree: go with it. If they split, show both options with rough cost and time, ask once, and follow the answer.

A film with motion-graphics titles or captions is still a **film** job.

## The brief (one per video)

Goal, length, format (9:16 or other), the post it belongs to, the offer line, tone, and any music, footage or people the user supplied. The team runs in its own subagents and returns a checked export recorded as `## Delivered video`. Use nothing else.

## Testing formats

Hold the hook constant when comparing film with motion (same first line and opening beat). Read hook rate (viewers past the first 3 s) and hold rate (watched to the end), but judge by signups.

## AI-content labels (check before relying)

Platforms ask you to label realistic AI-generated or heavily altered video, and rules change. Before posting, check the current setting on each platform (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube) and switch it on when the video uses AI people, voices or scenes. Tell the compliance reviewer which parts are AI.
