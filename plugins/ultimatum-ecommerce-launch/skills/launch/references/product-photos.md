# Product photos: shot list and setup

Hand the shot list and the house rules to the Website and Social teams in the skill args. Every number here is a rule of thumb, not a platform requirement; image size limits and recommended dimensions are **check before relying** on the platform's own help pages.

## House rules (decide once, write them in `brand/brand.md`)

| Decision | Pick one and keep it | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Pure white, or one colour, or one surface (wood, linen, concrete) | Mixed backgrounds make a catalogue look like a flea market |
| Crop ratio | Square (1:1) or portrait (4:5) | Grids line up; themes crop less |
| Product size in frame | Same margin on every main image (for example, product fills most of the frame with an even border) | Products look the same size in a collection grid |
| Light | Same direction and softness | Colour and shadow stay consistent |
| File names | `sku-angle-number.jpg`, e.g. `CND-CEDAR-front-01.jpg` | Easy bulk upload and matching to variants |

## Shot list per product

Shoot these in this order so nothing is missed. Tick them in the product sheet.

| # | Shot | What it answers | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Main / hero, front, on the house background | What is it? | This is the collection thumbnail; must read clearly on a phone |
| 2 | Back or side | What does the rest look like? | Same light and distance as #1 |
| 3 | Detail close-up | Is the quality real? | Texture, stitching, finish, label |
| 4 | Scale | How big is it? | In a hand, beside a common object, or on a model with height stated |
| 5 | In use / lifestyle | Can I picture owning it? | Real setting; one per product is enough at launch |
| 6 | Each colour or finish | Is the colour I pick the one I get? | One image per colour variant, linked to that variant |
| 7 | What is in the box | What arrives? | Everything laid flat, including inserts |
| 8 | Packaging (optional) | Is it gift-ready? | Useful for gifting stores |

Category extras:

| Category | Add |
|---|---|
| Clothing | On-model front and back, flat lay, fabric close-up, size label; state the model's height and size worn |
| Jewellery | On the body for scale, macro of clasp and stamp, the box |
| Food and drink | Ingredients panel readable, serving shot, nutrition label (labelling rules: check before relying) |
| Furniture, homeware | In a room for scale, dimensions overlay image, material close-up |
| Print-on-demand | The partner's mockups are fine to start, but order one sample and photograph it; mockups can differ from the real print |
| Digital products | Screenshots or page previews of the actual file, a "what you get" list image |

## Cheap setup that works

1. A window with indirect daylight, or a light tent with two lamps of the same colour temperature.
2. A sweep: a large sheet of white card curved from wall to table, so there is no horizon line.
3. A white card or foam board opposite the light to fill shadows.
4. Phone on a tripod, at the same height and distance for every product (mark the table with tape).
5. Lock focus and exposure on the phone; turn off portrait or beauty modes that blur or smooth the product.
6. Shoot a grey or white card in the first frame of each session to correct colour later.

## Editing pass

- Correct white balance from the reference frame; colours must match the real product. A wrong colour is a return waiting to happen.
- Crop to the house ratio and margin; straighten.
- Remove dust and background marks; do not remove real flaws, change shape or add features.
- Export as JPEG or WebP at a size large enough for zoom, then compress. Ask the Website team to check page weight in the browser check.
- Alt text: what the image shows in a few plain words, including colour, e.g. "Cedar candle in amber glass jar, side view".

## AI images and edits

- Background swaps and lifestyle scenes made with AI tools (Higgsfield and others) only after a credit check and the user's yes, one generation per yes.
- The product itself must stay accurate: shape, colour, size, label. An AI image that flatters the product beyond reality is a compliance blocker.
- No AI people presented as real customers or reviewers. Label AI content where the platform asks for it.
- Licences and commercial rights for AI tools: base plugin `licenses.md` (`ultimatum-launch`), check before relying.

## Beginner mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Every product shot on a different day, different light | Batch shoot the whole launch catalogue in one or two sessions |
| Only one photo per product | At least shots 1, 3, 4 and 5 before launch |
| Lifestyle shot as the main image | Main image is the product alone; lifestyle comes later in the gallery |
| Colour variants share one photo | One image per colour, attached to the variant |
| Huge uncompressed files | Compress before upload; check page speed on a phone |
| Supplier or competitor photos used without permission | Use only images you shot, commissioned or have written permission for; log the permission |
| Text baked into the main image | Keep text on the page, not in the photo; marketplaces and ad platforms may reject it (check before relying) |
