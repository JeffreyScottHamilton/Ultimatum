# Identity craft: later

Read when someone asks for one of these; each was left out on purpose.

- **Logo, icon and identity generators** (scripts that call paid image APIs): Ultimatum doesn't ship them. Use the user's own image tools with their yes (`ai-logo.md`).
- **Mockups** (cards on desks, logos on shopfronts, vehicles): make them only when the owner asks, with their tools; they're for presenting, not for the deliverable files.
- **Long colour-meaning lists by industry:** left out because the meanings are cultural and change; `logo-colour.md` says how to decide from the owner's market instead.
- **Trade show booths, promotional items, wayfinding systems:** follow the supplier's template; the rules in `identity-kit.md` (vector mark, one-colour version, printer's colours) still apply.
