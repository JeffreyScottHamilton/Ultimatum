# Licences and terms to check

Plain summaries, not legal advice. Items marked **check before relying** were not confirmed on the vendor's own page; confirm there before the user depends on them.

| Asset | What to know | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| GSAP | Free, including commercial use and all plugins. Not allowed in no-code visual builders that compete with Webflow. | Fine for client sites; flag only if the product is a visual builder. |
| Web fonts | Google Fonts faces are open licences (mostly OFL). Paid faces are licensed per site or per page view. | Self-host or link open faces freely; a paid face needs the owner's licence on record. |
| Stock photos (Unsplash, Pexels and similar) | Free for commercial use under each site's own licence (**check before relying**). Recognisable people, brands or private property may still need consent. | Log the source and link; prefer the owner's real photos. |
| Icon sets | Most popular sets are MIT, ISC or CC-BY; CC-BY needs a credit. | Note the set and its licence in the README. |
| AI images | Rights depend on the tool's plan; purely AI-made images may not be copyrightable in the US. | Tell the owner before they rely on owning them; never show AI people as real staff or customers. |
| Reviews and logos | Third-party reviews and client logos need permission and must be current. | Show only what the owner can back. |
