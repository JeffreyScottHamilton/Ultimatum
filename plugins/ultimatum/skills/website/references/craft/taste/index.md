# Taste craft: index

Read when a website build wants the full craft layer: one line per file, with the question it answers. Rebuilt in our own words from the MIT-licensed Taste skill (Leonxlnx/taste-skill); the house rules in `../../website/design-rules.md` still win on any conflict.

## Reading order

1. `principles.md` → `copy.md` → `voice.md` → `page-plans.md` → `system.md` → `layout-and-heroes.md` → `sections.md`.
2. Then only the files the plan needs, and `quality-gate.md` with `accessibility.md` and `performance.md` before hand-over.

## Files

- `principles.md`: What bar must the page clear, in what order is the work done, and what is never done?
- `copy.md`: How do I write every word of the page before any layout, and which tells give copy away?
- `voice.md`: Who is the buyer, how should this business sound, and how many devices may the copy use?
- `page-plans.md`: Which kind of site is this, how is it planned one line per section, and how are choices dealt?
- `system.md`: What tokens, type scale, spacing, grid and base styles does the stylesheet start from?
- `layout-and-heroes.md`: How tall is each section, how does content fit the grid, and which hero do I build?
- `sections.md`: How is each section after the hero built, including local-business and app sections?
- `place-pages.md`: What changes for a restaurant, shop, studio or anywhere people walk in?
- `cards-and-ui.md`: How are cards and pieces of a software product's interface drawn inside a section?
- `photos.md`: Which pictures, where from, how big, and how are they credited?
- `icons-and-diagrams.md`: Which logos and glyphs may I use, and how do I draw a connection diagram?
- `motion.md`: What moves, on which trigger, for how long, and what shows under reduced motion?
- `moments-and-scenes.md`: What are the page's one or two memorable moments, and when does a tech page get a scroll scene?
- `grounds-and-effects.md`: Which section backgrounds, brand band and (tech only) single effect does the page get?
- `effect-parts.md`: How does an effect live inside one element, such as a headline, ring, chart or feature-card panel?
- `objects.md`: When does a page show a modelled object or a 3D product render, and at what cost?
- `accessibility.md`: Which WCAG 2.2 AA checks must every craft move pass?
- `performance.md`: What are the byte, request, paint and WebGL budgets, and how do I measure them?
- `quality-gate.md`: Which checks, widths, rounds and waivers apply before a page is finished, and what goes in the report?
- `later.md`: Which kit pieces ship here, which source kit pieces, tools and video rules do not yet, and what would each take?

Data: the pattern table (grounds, effects, moments, hero layouts) is `../../../../../data/craft/taste/patterns.csv`.

Kit: our starter page, grounds, UI fragments, icons, diagrams and three effects are in `../../../../../data/craft/taste/kit/`; `later.md` § Shipped kit lists each with its check.
