# Build brief (Business, launch mode)

Fill the `## Build brief` section of `.ultimatum/brief.md`. Short lines; label guesses. Draw on `brand/brand.md` (voice, positioning) and the interview in `references/interview.md` when it is missing.

- **Audience:** who visits, in their own words if you have them.
- **Offer:** what they get, the price or "ask", and the one promise.
- **Main visitor action:** the single thing a visitor should do (book, buy, call, sign up).
- **Pages:** the fewest pages that get a visitor to that action.
- **Known business details:** name, place, hours, contact, prices, photos the user supplied. Only facts the user gave or you saw.
- **Open questions:** what the user still has to decide. Do not guess these into facts.
- **Checks that show it works:** e.g. "from the home page a phone user reaches the booking form in two taps", "the form sends", "each campaign link is tracked".

End with `Status: draft (not approved)`. The Launch Manager shows it to the user; `ult gate approve brief` refuses while Audience, Offer or Main visitor action is empty. If the brief changes after approval, the approval reads `changed` until the user approves the new version.
