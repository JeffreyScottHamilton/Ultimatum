# Custom direction: a page of its own

Read only when the user asks for a bespoke look ("custom", "no theme", "a world of its own") and when no brand or locked design file exists. Silence means pick a catalog theme (`themes.md`). Naming a theme, an existing brand, component scope or fast mode sends you back to the catalog path. For the first-pass brief and answers, see `principles.md` and `../../intake.md`.

## What stays yours

Nothing is dealt: no theme, shape, section list or palette recipe. Keep the floor (`principles.md`), the gates (`gates.md`) and the sweep. Aim for the page a design lead would show a client who has rejected every template.

- Unique means specific: subject, place, materials, numbers, how the people talk. Swap in a rival's name; if the line still works, go further.
- Clean means exact: few type sizes, set well apart; a single spacing rhythm; aligned edges; and, per screen, one element strong enough to justify the screen.
- Full means a whole site, usually 6–9 sections each built round something worth looking at (a working product, a photo, real facts set large, something to try). A head plus a paragraph is unfinished.
- Free means light or dark, a lone colour or four, type doing a picture's job, photos bleeding to the edges, a structure no template owns.

## Before any code

1. Learn the subject: name and meaning, place, what it does in one verb, real numbers, hours, what its world makes, the words customers use.
2. Park the page you would build by default, and its obvious twist. The usual suspects: split hero (text left, picture right), three numbered steps, hairline-divided label/value rows, a form in the closing band, an oversized wordmark above a dark footer, small grey labels, one careful accent on a pale ground. If a plan holds two of these, you have the template.
3. Sketch three directions far apart, two lines each (idea, structure, look). Build the one worth fighting for; borrow from the others only to strengthen it.
4. One idea runs the whole page, true of this client only, in type, colour, structure, motion or something the visitor does. If the idea ends at the hero, you have a template wearing a good hero.
5. Content shapes structure. Settle what a visitor has to see, grasp and do, in that order, then choose a form per part.

## Spent defaults, refused on sight

- A clay accent and a high-contrast serif on cream paper; near-black with one neon accent; pale ground with a timid accent; purple to blue; a hue chosen by category (blue for software, green for health).
- The reflex fonts (`fonts.md`).
- Centred head over three cards, or hero/features/CTA/footer; a default secondary button.
- A subject painted on: paper or grain as surface, stamps, dials, bevels, engravings.
- Eyebrows, mono labels, tracked capitals, a single tinted word in a headline, middle-dot strings. This matches Ultimatum's anti-generic lint (`../../website/design-rules.md` section 2).

## Pictures and words

Use pictures only if the subject can show something: place, food, people at work, an object. Photos few and large, credited (`imagery.md`). Software shows the product itself, built and working. A service or an idea can be carried by type and colour. Every picture passes the deletion test: remove it and the page loses a fact.

Copy is half the design (`craft-copy.md`): a fact in every heading, built from the owner's nouns, about 80 words a screen. Avoid the two-clipped-fragments headline. Headings read alone should tell this client's story.

## What still binds

The floor, every gate (a custom hero is free of the hero-composition gates), the facts rule, licensed pictures, the stamp, the sweep, both reviews, WCAG 2.2 contrast and focus. Not the layout catalog, genre file or shape catalog. For how states, slots and forms behave, see `patterns.md`; it never dictates looks.

## Mechanics

- Declare every colour and font role in the token file; set sizes, spacing and widths the design needs, keeping the names.
- One or two families, a third only as an outlier with one role; choose a display face no catalog theme uses (`fonts.md`).
- Colour in OKLCH under any posture, reason stated in the stamp; contrast and focus unchanged.
- In the picks block, replace Shape and Theme with Idea (a sentence) and Look (ground, ink, accent, faces with provenance).
- Stamp: `theme: custom`, the idea, a 4–8 word vibe, paper, accent, faces, hero in your own words, posture and axes (paper light/mid/dark; display class; accent warm/cool/neutral/chromatic-hue).

## Local business and app notes

A bespoke site for a local business still leads with the phone, address, hours and the one booking or quote action. A bespoke app marketing page still shows the real pricing and the sign-up path. Boldness lives in the idea and craft, never in hiding the action.
