# Startup numbers

Use when the user asks or when a decision depends on it. Rough is fine; label every figure **guessed** or **observed** (and the source). Never invent a source.

## Market size, two ways
- **Top-down:** a published total for the category, times the share you could reach (region, segment). Say where the total came from.
- **Bottom-up:** number of reachable buyers x price x purchases per year. Count real places you can find them (shops in the town, members of a group).
- If the two differ by more than 3x, say so and trust the bottom-up one until data proves otherwise.

## Simple model
- Revenue = price x buyers (per month).
- Costs = fixed (rent, tools, pay) + variable (materials, fees, ads) per sale.
- **Break-even** buyers = fixed costs / (price - variable cost per sale). Show it for the guessed and a pessimistic case.

## Metrics to watch (3-5)
Pick those that match the main action: visitors to action rate, cost to win one customer, repeat or retention rate, average order value, and monthly revenue against break-even. Name how each is measured and how often it is checked.

Keep a short table in the report: figure, value, guessed or observed, what would confirm it.
