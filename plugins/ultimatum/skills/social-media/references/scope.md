# Scope

Read at the start of a social job, before drafting anything.

Ask in rounds of up to four questions with AskUserQuestion, recommended answer first and a "Skip, use your pick" option. Skip anything `brand/brand.md`, the build brief or the user's message already answers, and say where each answer came from.

| # | Question | Options (recommended first) | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How big is the job? | a short campaign (one to two weeks) · one post · running a channel week to week | plan length; whether the results loop runs |
| 2 | Which channel first? | where the audience already is · Instagram · TikTok · YouTube Shorts · LinkedIn · Facebook · X · Pinterest | one channel is easier to read; more can follow |
| 3 | What format? | text with one image · carousel · short vertical video · text only | which limits and checks apply |
| 4 | What is the one action? | visit a page · book · sign up · buy · reply or message | every post points at it; links are tagged for it |
| 5 | When does the first post go out? | in one to two weeks · this week · a fixed date | how much is drafted before the first approval |
| 6 | Who posts it? | the user, from drafts · the user's scheduler · a connected account they authorize | decides the hand-over in `publish.md` |
| 7 | Anything paid, gifted or affiliate? | no · yes (say which posts) | disclosure rules in `review.md` |

Default when the user has no view: a two-week campaign on the one channel their audience uses, two or three posts a week, drafts handed over for them to post.

## Video

If the format is video, say plainly that this skill writes the script, the on-screen lines and the caption, and checks the exported file; it does not choose a visual style or edit. Ask whether they will film it themselves or already have footage, and which app they edit in, only to write a script they can follow. Then use the hook video script in `writing.md`.

## What to write down

Put the answers at the top of `social/plan.json` (`"scope": {...}`) and the audience, voice and offer in `brand/brand.md` if they are new, so later sessions do not ask again.
