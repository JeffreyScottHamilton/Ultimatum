# Design review and QA

Read when you need to audit a design, review a plan, or test a site, API or job. Pick a file by question.

- `design-review-modes.md`: which review to run, what inputs it needs, how deep to go?
- `first-impression.md`: what does a page say in the first five seconds, and what are quick wins?
- `page-modes.md`: is the page Persuade, Operate, Read or Experience, and which hard rejections and checks apply?
- `checklist-visual.md`: what to inspect for layout, type, colour, spacing and imagery?
- `checklist-behaviour.md`: what to inspect for interaction, motion, responsive, copy and accessibility?
- `slop-tells.md`: which patterns make a page look machine-made, and how to grade them?
- `page-lint.md`: which checkable tells does a built page show, by script, before the review?
- `render-check.md`: what does the built page measure in a real browser at eight widths (overflow, wrapping, contrast, the first screen)?
- `design-audit-recipes.md`: how do I measure the checkable rules with a Playwright script?
- `flows-and-consistency.md`: how do I review a multi-step flow and consistency across pages?
- `scoring-and-baseline.md`: how are scores, the baseline file and regressions worked out?
- `fix-loop.md`: how do I triage and fix design findings safely?
- `craft-audit.md`: what must a built page get right (claims, sample content, ship floor, motion)?
- `build-loop.md`: how do I build a set of items in rounds with receipts?
- `qa-taxonomy.md`: what severity and category does a bug get, and what is walked on each page?
- `qa-modes-and-loop.md`: how do I plan a QA run, stay safe, and probe step by step?
- `qa-health-score.md`: how is a QA health score computed, and what does the framework change?
- `qa-playwright-recipes.md`: how do I sweep a local page for links, dead buttons and console errors?
- `qa-functional.md`: how do I test an API, command, job or webhook?
- `qa-reports.md`: what do the QA reports contain?
- `qa-fix-and-tests.md`: how do I fix QA findings and add regression tests?
- `plan-review-passes.md`: how do I review a plan's design before the build?
- `plan-review-outputs.md`: what goes in the plan review's output and score?
- `second-opinion.md`: how do I get an independent design review?
- `later.md`: what from the source is parked, and why?

Data tables are in `../../../../../data/craft/design-review-qa/` (see its `README.md`).
