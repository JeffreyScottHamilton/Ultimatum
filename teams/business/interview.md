# Brand interview

Ten questions in three groups. Ask one group at a time, skip what the user's files already answer, and mark unanswered items `(guess)` in `brand/brand.md`.

## Group 1: the business
1. What do you sell, and what does one customer get?
2. Why did you start it, in a sentence?
3. What is the price or price range, and how is it charged?

## Group 2: the customer and the market
4. Who is your best customer today? Describe one real person.
5. What did they do before they found you, or what would they do without you?
6. Which two or three competitors or substitutes do they compare you with?
7. Why do customers pick you, in their words? What makes them hesitate?

## Group 3: the feel
8. Which brands or sites do you like, and what do you like about each?
9. What must the brand never sound or look like?
10. Give three adjectives for how customers should feel about you.

The three adjectives become the **Voice** line. Check that they fit together (warm, direct and funny is workable; luxurious and cheap is not) and that each shows up in the sample headline.
