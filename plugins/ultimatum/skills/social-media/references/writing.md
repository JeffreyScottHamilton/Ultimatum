# Writing

Read when drafting posts, captions or a video script.

## Every post

- **Hook in the first line.** The feed cuts captions near 125 characters; the reason to stop must fit before that.
- **One idea, one next step.** The last line asks for the one action from scope, matching the link.
- **Name the hook type** in the plan (`hook_type`), so results compare like with like: result first, a mistake people make, a question customers ask, a number, a quick demo, behind the scenes, before and after, a myth corrected, a short story.
- **Plain words** the owner would say aloud. Claims only from the brief or the user; no made-up numbers, quotes or reviews.
- **Write two or three hooks** for the same idea and log each with `ult posts variant add <id> --hook-type <type> --text "..."`.

## Captions and hashtags

- Keep within the platform limit (`ult posts specs <channel>`); a scheduler or API can stop earlier than the app.
- Hashtags: a few specific ones beat many broad ones; several platforms now cap them. Write multi-word tags in CamelCase (`#BikeRepair`) so screen readers say the words.
- Mentions and tags of other accounts only with their permission when it implies a partnership.

## Links

One destination per post, tagged: `utm_source=<channel>&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=<name>`, plus `utm_content=<post id>` to tell posts apart. Where the platform has no clickable links in captions, say "link in bio" and put the tagged link there. Open every link before showing the batch.

## Alt text and captions on video

Every image gets alt text: what is in it and why it matters to the post, under about 125 characters, no "image of". Every video with speech gets captions; burned-in or the platform's own. Text on images stays short and high-contrast.

## Hook video script (15 to 45 seconds, 9:16)

```
Hook (0-2 s): <line on screen and spoken: a question, a number, or the result first>
Proof (2-10 s): <what is shown: the product working, a before and after, a real moment>
Point (10-25 s): <one idea, said plainly>
Next step (last 3 s): <one action, matching the link>
On-screen text: <short lines, each readable in under 2 seconds>
Caption: <first line repeats the hook>
Sound: <voiceover, or a sound the user picks inside the app; licensed music only>
```

This is the script only. Filming is the user's; for the cut, captions and look, see `video.md` and `styles.md`.

## Blog or article for social

One question a customer types into search as the heading; answer it in the first two or three sentences; then steps, examples and real numbers from the brief; one link to the main action in context and one near the end; title under about 60 characters.

## Tells to strip

Openers such as "In today's fast-paced world" and "Let's dive in"; "game-changer", "unlock", "seamless", "elevate"; three adjectives in a row; "It's not just X, it's Y"; stacked rhetorical questions; an emoji on every line; dashes used as filler; a closing line that repeats the point. Read it aloud: if the owner would not say it, rewrite it.
