# Pages and SEO

Use the relevant parts when you build the site (`website/guide.md`).

## Every page

- One clear primary action, visible without scrolling on a phone: a tap-to-call button, a "Book" button, or a short enquiry form. Same wording everywhere ("Book a table", not three variants).
- Name, area and the service in the first screen. A visitor should know what, where, and what to do within five seconds (rule of thumb).
- Phone number as a tap link and in the header on mobile.
- A unique title (rule of thumb: under about 60 characters) and meta description (about 155), and a social preview image.

## Page by page

**Home.** Headline saying what you do and for whom, in the visitor's words. Primary button. Three to six services as short cards linking on. One strong proof item (a review with name and a photo). Hours, area and address. A closing action.

**Services.** What is included, for whom, how it works in 3-4 steps, price or "from" price if the owner agrees (check prices with the owner; never invent), a short FAQ, the action. One page per main service when there is real content for each.

**About.** The person or team, real photos, how long, why they do it, credentials and licences. Trust comes from specifics: years, places, names, numbers that are true.

**Proof.** Reviews with names and dates (only real ones, with permission where needed), before-and-after or portfolio work with a line of context each, logos or client names with consent, awards, press, accreditations. Link to the review platform they came from.

**Contact or booking.** The action only: booking embed or short form (name, contact, what you need; avoid extra fields), phone, address, map, hours, parking or access, expected reply time. A confirmation message after submitting.

**Optional.** Service-area pages, FAQ, pricing, blog or news. Add only with real content.

## Trust signals

Real photos of the real place and people; reviews; licences, insurance or registration numbers where the trade requires it (the owner supplies them); guarantees only if the owner will honour them; visible contact details; a privacy note if data is collected. Compliance review checks every claim ("best", "guaranteed", health or legal outcomes).

## Local SEO basics

Rules of thumb; search engines change, so check before relying on any specific ranking claim.

- **Google Business Profile.** Claim or verify it; the owner does the verification. Fill category, services, hours, photos, website link, booking link. Any change to a live listing needs the user's yes first.
- **Consistent name, address, phone (NAP).** Same spelling and format on the site, the profile, directories and social pages.
- **Service-area pages.** One per town or area only when each has distinct, true content (local examples, local reviews); copy-and-swap pages are thin and can hurt.
- **Reviews.** Ask recent customers directly after a good job with a link to the profile; never offer payment, never write fake ones, never gate reviews to happy customers only (platform rules: check before relying).
- **Listings.** The main directories for the trade and area, with the same NAP.
- **Page basics.** One H1 per page naming service and place naturally; descriptive image alt text; internal links between services; a sitemap and a robots file; fast on mobile.

## Schema markup basics

Add JSON-LD structured data, with true values only: `LocalBusiness` (or a subtype such as `Restaurant`, `HairSalon`, `Dentist`) with name, address, phone, hours, geo, url, image and `sameAs` social links; `Service` for key services; `FAQPage` only where the page shows those questions; `Review` markup only for reviews shown on the page and about the business itself (guidelines: check before relying). Test with Google's Rich Results Test and fix errors.

## Accessibility basics

- Text contrast of at least 4.5:1 for body text; check buttons and text over images.
- Tap targets large enough for a thumb (about 44 px, rule of thumb).
- Every image has alt text (or empty alt if decorative); every form field has a visible label.
- Everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline; headings in order.
- Respect reduced-motion settings; no autoplay sound.
- Captions on video; opening hours and the booking path available as text, not only inside images.
- Link text says where it goes ("Book a haircut"), not "click here".
