# After the event

The week after decides whether the next event starts with a warm list or from zero. Every send needs the organiser's yes.

## Within 48 hours

- **Thank-you message** to everyone who attended: one or two warm lines, a highlight, the feedback link, and what comes next (the next date, a course, a way to stay in touch).
- **A separate note to no-shows**: no blame; the recording or slides if available, and the next date.
- **Thanks to helpers, speakers, partners and the venue**, personally.
- **Photos** shared with attendees only where everyone identifiable agreed.

## Feedback form

Short: five questions or fewer, most of them one tap.

1. How would you rate the event overall? (1-5)
2. What was the best part? (free text)
3. What should we change? (free text)
4. How did you hear about it? (the channels from the plan, plus "other")
5. Would you come to another? (yes, maybe, no) and may we email you about it? (unticked by default)

Ask permission before quoting any answer in public, and quote it word for word.

## Recordings

- Send a recording only if speakers and attendees were told and agreed (`day-of.md`).
- Cut or blur anyone who opted out.
- Share by link with a clear note on who may watch it; decide with the organiser whether it is for attendees only.

## Turning attendees into the next list

- Add people who gave marketing consent to the organiser's list, tagged with the event.
- Do not add people who did not consent; the sign-up tool's list is for this event only.
- Keep the export out of Git and chat logs.
- Ask the most engaged attendees whether they would help spread the word next time.

## Results review

Pull numbers from the sign-up tool, the payment provider and the platforms. Write "not available" for anything you cannot read; never estimate.

| Number | How to get it |
|---|---|
| Registered | Sign-ups or tickets sold, including free places |
| Attended | Check-ins |
| No-show rate | (registered minus attended) divided by registered |
| Revenue | Ticket income after refunds; note platform and payment fees separately |
| Cost | Venue, materials, food, promotion, tools, helpers |
| Cost per attendee | Cost divided by attended |
| Sign-up sources | Tracked links and the "how did you hear" answers |
| Waitlist size | People who wanted a place but did not get one |
| Feedback score | Average of question 1, and the themes in the free text |

Record the review with:

```
ult social --results "<registered, attended, revenue, top source, feedback score>"
```

Add the learnings to `brand/brand.md` so the next event starts from them.

## What to repeat or change

Go through the numbers with the organiser and pick at most three changes for next time:

| What you see | Consider |
|---|---|
| Sold out early with a waitlist | A larger room, a second date, or a higher price |
| Many free sign-ups, high no-show | A small fee or deposit, or an extra reminder |
| Most sign-ups from one channel | Start there earlier next time; drop channels that brought none |
| Good attendance, low feedback | Fix the content or the format before promoting harder |
| Costs above revenue | Fewer paid extras, a higher price, or a sponsor |
| Last-minute rush | Announce earlier, or use a real early-bird |

## Beginner mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Waiting two weeks to say thank you | Send within 48 hours while it is fresh |
| Emailing every registrant about future events | Only those who agreed |
| Posting crowd photos without consent | Use only consented shots |
| Judging success by feel | Write the numbers down; compare next time |
| Changing everything for the next event | Keep what worked; change one to three things |
