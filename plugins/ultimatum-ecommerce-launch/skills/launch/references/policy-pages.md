# Policy pages: outlines

Four pages must be live before launch: refund and returns, shipping, privacy, terms. These outlines say what each page should answer; they are not legal text and not legal advice. Required wording, cooling-off periods, consumer rights and privacy duties differ by country and state: **check before relying** on the rules where the owner and the customers are, and use a lawyer for anything beyond a template. The owner signs off every page.

## How to produce them

1. Start from the platform's policy generator or a reputable template (Shopify and WooCommerce both offer starting points; check before relying on what they cover).
2. Fill every bracket with the store's real facts, using the outlines below as a checklist. Facts come from the intake answers, not guesses; any `(guess)` stays visible until the owner confirms it.
3. Match reality: the privacy page lists the apps actually installed; the shipping page matches the rates actually set.
4. Run this plugin's `ultimatum-ecommerce-launch:compliance-reviewer` agent on all four pages and on any product page that repeats a promise from them.
5. Show the owner each page; record the yes. Link all four from the footer and from checkout where the platform allows.
6. Put a "last updated" date on each page; review it when the store changes apps, carriers or regions.

## Refund and returns

- Who can return, and within how many days of delivery (a rule of thumb many small stores use is 30 days; some places set a legal minimum: check before relying).
- Condition required: unused, tags on, original packaging.
- Items that cannot be returned: personalised, perishable, hygiene items, digital downloads, final sale. State why.
- How to start a return: email or form, what to include (order number, photos for damage).
- Who pays return shipping, and when the store pays (damaged, wrong item).
- How refunds are paid: original payment method, store credit, or exchange; how long after the item arrives.
- Damaged or wrong items: how to report, and the time limit for reporting.
- Exchanges: allowed or not, and how.
- Contact for questions.

## Shipping

- Where you ship (countries, regions) and where you do not.
- Processing time before dispatch (handling time), and whether made-to-order items take longer.
- Methods, typical delivery times and costs, or a table; free-shipping threshold if any.
- Tracking: when and how it is sent.
- International orders: duties and import taxes, and who pays them.
- Lost, late or stolen parcels: what the store does and when to contact it.
- Wrong address entered by the customer: what happens.
- Peak-season delay note, if relevant.

## Privacy

- Who runs the store and how to contact them about data.
- What personal data is collected: name, address, email, phone, payment details (handled by the payment provider), order history, device and cookie data.
- Why each is used: fulfilling orders, support, marketing with consent, fraud prevention, analytics.
- Who it is shared with: platform, payment provider, shipping carriers, email tool, analytics, ad platforms, review app. List the real ones; check every installed app.
- Cookies and tracking: what is set, and the consent choices offered where required.
- How long data is kept.
- The customer's rights: access, correction, deletion, opting out of marketing; how to ask.
- International transfers, if the vendors are in other countries.
- Children: whether the store is aimed at them (usually not).

## Terms of service

- Who the seller is and how to contact them.
- How an order becomes a contract (when it is accepted), and the right to cancel orders.
- Prices, currency, taxes, and what happens if a price is listed in error.
- Payment methods.
- Product descriptions and images: reasonable care, colour may vary slightly on screens.
- Links to the shipping and refund pages rather than repeating them.
- Limits of liability, warranties (as local law allows).
- Intellectual property of site content.
- Governing law and how disputes are handled.
- Changes to the terms.

## Other pages worth having

| Page | Contents |

## Beginner mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Copying another store's policies | Write from the store's own facts; a copied page promises things you do not do |
| Policy says 30 days, product page says 14 | One source: link to the policy page instead of restating it |
| Privacy page lists no apps | Go through every installed app and email tool and list what it receives |
| "No refunds" with no exceptions | Many places give consumers rights a store cannot remove: check before relying, and have the owner decide with advice |
| Pages live but not linked | Footer link to each, and from checkout |
| Pages never updated | Review when adding an app, carrier, country or product type |
