# Event route

Choose the path once the brief is approved. Tool names below are examples, not recommendations; fees, features and terms are **check before relying** on each vendor's own page, and a fact-finder marks each fact observed (with link) or guessed.

## By lead time

| Days to the event | Plan | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 8 weeks or more | Full plan in `timeline.md` | Room for line-up reveals, an early-bird window and partner outreach |
| 4 to 8 weeks | Full plan, starting at the 4-week window, with the 8-week tasks done in the first few days | Announce within the first week |
| 2 to 4 weeks | Compressed plan in `timeline.md` | Lean on the existing list and partners; skip early-bird |
| Under 2 weeks | Compressed plan, own list and direct invitations only | Tell the user plainly what was cut and why |

## By event type

| Type | Main sign-up | Page | Video style | Watch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Workshop or class | Paid ticket, small capacity, waitlist | Own page if a site exists; listing platform otherwise | Own footage of a past session, or brand film | Materials, skill level, what people take home |
| Talk or meetup | Free sign-up | Listing platform or community page | Short own-footage clip of the speaker | No-show rate is often high for free events; plan reminders |
| Show or performance | Paid ticket, possibly tiers or seating | Own page plus a listing platform | Brand film or performance clips (with performers' consent) | Rights to recorded music in promo clips |
| Opening or pop-up | Free, optional RSVP | Own page or social post; local listings | Brand film of the space; carousel of what is on offer | Weather and footfall; an RSVP helps estimate numbers |
| Webinar | Free registration | Own page or the webinar tool's page | Product demo or motion graphics | Time zones; send the recording to registrants |
| Fundraiser | Ticket or donation | Own page; the cause's channels | Brand film of the people helped, with consent | Clear statement of where money goes; donation rules: check locally |
| Small conference | Tiered tickets | Own page | Motion graphics for line-up reveals; speaker clips | Speaker confirmations before reveals |

## Sign-up tool

Pick the simplest tool that does what the event needs.

| Need | Tool type | Examples (check before relying) |
|---|---|---|
| Free sign-up, small group | A form plus a confirmation email | The site's form builder, a form tool |
| Paid tickets, one price | Ticketing platform or the site's checkout | Event ticketing platforms, the shop's checkout |
| Tiers, seating, check-in app | Full ticketing platform | Choose one that supports tiers and on-the-day check-in |
| Online event | Webinar or meeting tool with registration | A webinar tool that sends reminders and the recording |
| Donations | Donation or fundraising tool | One that issues receipts where the organiser needs them |

Check the fee model, payout timing, refund handling and who owns the attendee list before choosing. The attendee list should be exportable; it becomes the next event's list.

## Own page or listing platform

| | Own page (on the organiser's site) | Listing platform |
|---|---|---|
| Strengths | Brand control, the organiser owns traffic and data, reusable for the next event | Built-in discovery, quick setup, checkout included |
| Weaknesses | Needs a site and a sign-up tool; no built-in audience | Fees, platform rules, less control over the list and the page |
| Pick when | A site exists and the audience comes from the organiser's own list and channels | No site, or the audience browses listings in that city or topic |

Both is common: the own page as the main link, the listing as a second door with the same details. Keep one source of truth for date, time, price and capacity.

## Team hand-offs

- Business (`business/guide.md`): positioning line, who it is for, the one promise.
- Website (`website/guide.md`): the event page (what, when, where, who for, what you leave with, price, the sign-up button, refund line, accessibility, FAQ). A single page is enough for most events.
- Social media (`social-media/guide.md`): countdown posts from `promotion.md`; its scope file picks the video style.
