# Custom direction: a page of its own

Read only when the user asks for a bespoke look ("custom", "no theme", "a world of its own") and when no brand or locked design file exists. Silence means pick a catalog theme (`themes.md`). Naming a theme, an existing brand, component scope or fast mode sends you back to the catalog path. For the first-pass brief and answers, see `principles.md` and `../../intake.md`.

## What stays yours

Nothing is dealt: no theme, shape, section list or palette recipe. Keep the floor (`principles.md`), the gates (`gates.md`) and the sweep. Aim for the page a design lead would show a client who has rejected every template.

- Unique means specific: subject, place, materials, numbers, how the people talk. Put the nearest competitor's name on it; if it still works, go further.
- Clean means exact: a few type sizes far apart, one spacing rhythm, aligned edges, and one thing on every screen strong enough to be its reason.
- Full means a whole site, usually 6–9 sections each built round something worth looking at (a working product, a photo, real facts set large, something to try). A head plus a paragraph is unfinished.
- Free means dark or light, one colour or four, type as picture or photos edge to edge, a structure no template has.

## Before any code

1. Learn the subject: name and meaning, place, what it does in one verb, real numbers, hours, what its world makes, the words customers use.
2. Set aside the page you would make anyway and its obvious twist. The usual suspects: split hero (text left, picture right), three numbered steps, label/value rows between hairlines, a closing band with a form, dark footer under a huge wordmark, small grey labels, a pale ground with one careful accent. Two of those in one plan means you have the template.
3. Sketch three directions far apart, two lines each (idea, structure, look). Build the one worth fighting for; borrow from the others only to strengthen it.
4. One idea runs the whole page, true of this client only, in type, colour, structure, motion or something the visitor does. An idea that stops at the hero is a template with a good hero.
5. Content builds the structure: decide what a visitor must see, understand and do, in order, then pick the form for each part.

## Spent defaults, refused on sight

- Cream paper with a high-contrast serif and a clay accent; near-black with one neon accent; pale ground with a timid accent; purple to blue; a hue chosen by category (blue for software, green for health).
- The reflex fonts (`fonts.md`).
- Centred head over three cards, or hero/features/CTA/footer; a stock second action.
- A subject painted on: paper or grain as surface, stamps, dials, bevels, engravings.
- Eyebrows, tracked capitals, mono labels, one coloured word in a headline, middle-dot strings. This matches Ultimatum's anti-generic lint (`../../website/design-rules.md` section 2).

## Pictures and words

Pictures only when the subject has something to show: place, food, people at work, an object. Photos few and large, credited (`imagery.md`). Software shows the product itself, built and working. A service or an idea can be carried by type and colour. Every picture passes the deletion test: remove it and the page loses a fact.

Copy is half the design (`craft-copy.md`): the owner's own nouns, a fact in every heading, about 80 words a screen. Avoid the two-clipped-fragments headline. Read the headings alone; they should tell this client's story.

## What still binds

The floor, every gate (a custom hero is free of the hero-composition gates), the facts rule, licensed pictures, the stamp, the sweep, both reviews, WCAG 2.2 contrast and focus. Not the layout catalog, genre file or shape catalog. `patterns.md` shows how states, slots and forms work, not how they look.

## Mechanics

- Declare every colour and font role in the token file; set sizes, spacing and widths the design needs, keeping the names.
- One or two families, a third only as an outlier with one role; the display face is one no catalog theme uses (`fonts.md`).
- Colour in OKLCH under any posture, reason stated in the stamp; contrast and focus unchanged.
- Picks block: Shape and Theme become Idea (one sentence) and Look (ground, ink, accent, faces with provenance).
- Stamp: `theme: custom`, the idea, a 4–8 word vibe, paper, accent, faces, hero in your own words, posture and axes (paper light/mid/dark; display class; accent warm/cool/neutral/chromatic-hue).

## Local business and app notes

A bespoke site for a local business still leads with the phone, address, hours and the one booking or quote action. A bespoke app marketing page still shows the real pricing and the sign-up path. Boldness lives in the idea and craft, never in hiding the action.
