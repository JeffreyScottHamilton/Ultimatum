# Creator Cut: hooks and the paper edit

The paper edit is the whole short written as text before anything is rendered. Changing a line on paper is free; changing a render is not.

## Finding the short inside the recording

Read the transcript and mark lines that could stand alone:

- a claim the viewer might disagree with,
- a number or a detail that surprises,
- a mistake and what it taught,
- a before and after,
- a question the audience asks often, answered in one breath,
- a short story that ends on a turn.

Each mark gets in and out times and a one-line reason. One short holds one idea. A second good idea is a second short.

## The first two seconds

Viewers decide almost at once. The opening line must make sense with no context and give a reason to stay.

| Hook shape | Pattern (write your own line) |
|---|---|
| Claim | State the surprising conclusion first, then earn it |
| Mistake | Name the thing people get wrong, then the fix |
| Result | Show or say the outcome, then how it happened |
| Question | Ask what the viewer is already wondering |
| List | Promise a small number of things and deliver all of them |
| Contrast | "Most people do X. Do Y." |

- Open on the strongest line even if it was said last. Move that take to the top of the plan (a cold open), then cut back to the build-up.
- Never open on a greeting, a name, a logo or a breath.
- Put the hook on screen as text as well; many people watch with the sound off.
- The hook must be true and the short must pay it off. A hook the video never delivers is a blocker.

## The paper edit file (`cut/<name>.md`)

```
Short: <name>   Platform: <p>   Target: <15|30|60> s
Hook (0-2 s): "<line>"   from <take> <in>-<out>
1. "<line>"   <take> <in>-<out>   why: <reason>
2. ...
Last line: "<the one next step>"
On-screen text: <hook text>, <any key number>
B-roll ideas: <what the words describe>
```

Count the words: about 2.5 to 3 spoken words per second fits a creator pace. Over the target length means cutting a line, not speeding up the voice.

## Ending

- End when the point lands. Cut the sign-off that repeats the hook.
- One next step, said and shown: follow for part two, comment a word, visit the link. Not three.
- A short that loops (the last line leads back into the first) can be rewatched without a seam. Use it only when the words really connect.
