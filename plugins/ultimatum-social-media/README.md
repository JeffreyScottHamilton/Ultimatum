# Ultimatum Social Media

Plans and makes social content for one post, a short campaign or an ongoing channel. It works from your brand file, makes video in several styles and carousels or posts, and can hand a finished timeline to your editor. Nothing is posted, scheduled or paid for without your approval.

## Install

```
/plugin marketplace add JeffreyScottHamilton/Ultimatum
/plugin install ultimatum-social-media@ultimatum
```

## Start

Say "make a short video from my interview clips" or "plan a month of posts for my studio" in chat, or type `/ultimatum-social-media:social-media <what you want>`. Progress is kept in the project, so you can stop and resume.

## What it covers

- Scope questions in rounds of 4 until the scope is clear: one post, a campaign or a channel; format; platforms; whether you have footage; your editor; the deadline.
- Running a channel: content pillars, a posting rate you can keep, a four-week calendar and a weekly results review that changes one thing at a time.
- Video styles: your own footage (selecting takes from a transcript, cutting, captions, music under speech), product demos (script, recording, zooms, what to blur), motion graphics and brand film.
- Carousels and single images: slide structure, text sizes for phones, brand use and export sizes.
- Editors: DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro and Final Cut Pro, through live control where you have set it up, or a timeline file (FCPXML, OTIO or EDL) and a hand-over folder.
- Review by a compliance reviewer and a taste critic before anything is shown for approval.

## What it does not do

- It does not install editor plug-ins or MCP servers for you. If you want live control of an editor, you set that up yourself.
- It does not post, schedule, send or spend on its own. Each approval covers one batch or one action.
- It does not store credentials or media in Git.

Platform sizes, limits and third-party tool terms in the guides are marked "check before relying". Confirm them on the platform's or vendor's own page.
